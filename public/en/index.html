01 · About

About me

Mechanical engineer on loan to artificial intelligence.

I graduated in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Brescia with a thesis on data from fitness-tracking apps, used to size the battery pack of a self-balancing wheelchair for people with disabilities. Then came an internship at an aluminium foundry, between mechanical design and computational fluid dynamics.

Meanwhile, artificial intelligence kept taking up more of my time. In 2023 I co-founded StudyBuddy, the all-in-one app for university students, and my team and I won the NOI Hackathon by building CabChat, a voice interface for taxi drivers, in 24 hours.

Today I develop autonomous AI agents for businesses as part of KPS Financial Lab's MicroLabX project. As an AIFIA-certified trainer, I bring generative AI to companies and high schools: where it comes from, which tools to use and how to put it to work in everyday study and work.

Here I write about what I learn along the way, mostly in Italian.
