2026 · GT racing in the browser

Cubo Corse

A voxel GT racing game that runs in the browser, built with AI agents. Three tracks, including a 1:1 Monza rebuilt from OpenStreetMap data and terrain elevation, AI opponents, time of day and weather, time trial with a ghost of your best lap, and a photo mode.

On a computer, with keyboard and mouse. A dedicated graphics card helps. The game's menus are in Italian.
